8. Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Takeover Table open to everyone?

Registration is open, but attendance is limited. Every application and payment will be reviewed before final confirmation.

What does my registration cover?

Registration covers entry, the banquet, all scheduled conversations, the curated networking experience and the official guest package.

When will I receive the venue address?

The exact venue will be shared privately with confirmed guests before the event.

Is accommodation included?

No. Guests travelling into Lagos will arrange their transportation and accommodation separately.

Is my ticket transferable?

Transfers require written approval from the organising team and must be requested before the stated deadline.

Will registration be available at the venue?

No. All guests must register and receive confirmation before the event.

What should I wear?

The dress code is Formal Elegance. Additional style guidance will be sent to confirmed guests.

What happens if early-access tickets sell out before the deadline?

Registration will move immediately to the standard rate.
